About Agriculture.

Agriculture is the science, art, and practice of cultivating crops and raising animals to produce food, fibre, fuel, and other useful products. It is one of the oldest and most important activities in human civilization.

Agriculture provides essential food such as rice, wheat, maize, pulses, fruits, and vegetables.

It also provides raw materials for industries such as textiles, sugar, dairy, paper, and food processing.

Agriculture plays an important role in the economic development of a country by providing employment and income to millions of people, particularly in rural areas. It includes many activities such as crop production, horticulture, animal husbandry, dairy farming, poultry farming, fisheries, forestry, irrigation, and soil management.

Modern agriculture uses scientific methods and technologies such as tractors, improved seeds, fertilizers, irrigation systems, drones, sensors, satellites, biotechnology, and artificial intelligence to increase crop production and reduce losses.



Sustainable agriculture focuses on producing food while protecting soil, water, natural resources, and the environment. Therefore, agriculture is essential for food security, employment, rural development, industrial growth, and the overall development of society.
